# Instructions

- Use luxury minimalism, warm tactile surfaces, and product-led editorial layouts.
- Express Persian identity subtly through materials, rice, vessels, and typography.
- Prefer fewer, stronger selling sections.
- Favor packaging, rice macro, serving bowls, clean still life, and warm natural light.
- Reject unrelated landscapes, generic lifestyle stock, heavy darkness, and decorative grain overlays.

# Boundaries

Do not use this skill for backend logic or to justify visual elements that do not
improve product recognition, trust, or conversion.
