# Instructions

- Write natural, direct, premium Persian without exaggerated poetry.
- Communicate northern origin, clear provenance, careful selection, clean
  packaging, tracked delivery, and family-brand trust.
- Use clear collection labels such as مصرف روزانه، انتخاب سرآشپز، برداشت محدود،
  ذخیره اعلا، and مناسب رستوران.
- Keep English to small secondary labels.

# Boundaries

Do not use vague slogans where product facts, prices, or actions are needed.
