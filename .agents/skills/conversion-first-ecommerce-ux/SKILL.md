# Instructions

- Show products within the first two scrolls and enable add-to-cart within seconds.
- Answer what Keyvan sells, why it is trusted, what is available, and how to buy.
- Product cards must expose name, type, region, per-kilo price, pack total, pack
  size, aroma, recommended use, badge, add-to-cart, quick view, and wishlist.
- Use explicit pricing such as `۱۸۵٬۰۰۰ تومان / کیلو` and
  `۹۲۵٬۰۰۰ تومان / کیسه ۵ کیلویی`.
- Use origin, quality test, aroma/cooking guarantee, tracked shipping, direct
  support, and clean packaging as trust signals.

# Boundaries

Do not remove working commerce state or add decorative steps to the buying path.
