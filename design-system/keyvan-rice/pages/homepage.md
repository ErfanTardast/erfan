# Homepage Override

## Purpose

The homepage is a premium gateway, not the full storefront. A visitor should
understand what Keyvan sells and reach the shop within seconds.

## Structure

1. Compact commerce-aware header
2. One full-screen packaging hero with direct Persian copy, trust facts, and shop CTA
3. Minimal footer

The hero is the landing page. Newsletter, brand-story sections, trust bands,
collection indexes, product grids, repeated CTA sections, and cinematic motion
do not belong on the homepage.

## Interaction

- One primary hero action: `ورود به فروشگاه`
- Search, cart, and mobile menu remain available.
- Load overlays only after the user invokes them.
- Use native opacity/transform motion only and honor reduced motion.
